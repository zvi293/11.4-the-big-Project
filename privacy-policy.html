01Who I Am

This site is run by Zvi Moshe, operating as Zstore AI ("I", "me", "my"), an independent web studio based in Tel Aviv, Israel, working with clients in the United States and worldwide. For the personal data described here, I am the data controller. This policy explains, in plain language, what is collected on this site, why, and what you can do about it.

02What I Collect

Only what you type into the contact form:

  • Name — so I know how to address you.
  • Email address — so I can reply.
  • Your message, and the interest chip you optionally pick — so I understand what you need.

There is no account system, no newsletter, no tracking pixels and no analytics. Like almost every website, the hosting platform (Netlify) keeps short-lived technical server logs — for example IP addresses — for security and operations; I do not use them to identify visitors.

03How It Is Used

Your details are used only to:

  • Read and answer your message.
  • Discuss and, if we go ahead, run your project.

Nothing is sold, rented, traded or used for advertising. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is your consent (you chose to write to me) and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries about my work.

04Where It Is Processed

Form submissions are handled by Netlify (Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, USA), which hosts this site and stores each submission, and are then delivered to my mailbox, which is provided by Google (Gmail). Both companies act as processors with their own security and privacy commitments. Data may be shared beyond that only if the law requires it, or if it is necessary to protect someone's rights or safety.

05Retention

I keep messages for as long as the conversation, or the project that grows out of it, stays relevant — and then as ordinary business correspondence. If you want a message deleted earlier, email me and I will remove it from the form storage and my mailbox within 30 days.

06Your Rights

Wherever you are, you can ask me at any time to:

  • See the personal data I hold about you (access).
  • Fix anything that is wrong (correction).
  • Delete it (erasure).
  • Stop a particular use of it (objection / restriction).

These match the rights in the EU/UK GDPR, and for California residents the CCPA/CPRA — note that I do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it. To exercise any of them, just email me; I will answer within 30 days and will never treat you differently for asking.

07Cookies & Local Storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party analytics. Two small values may be saved by your own browser, on your device only, and are never sent anywhere:

  • zs-motion — remembers whether you switched the page motion off or on.
  • zs_last_submit — remembers for a few seconds that the form was just sent, to prevent accidental double-sending. It clears when the tab closes.

08Security

The site is served exclusively over HTTPS with modern security headers, and the form submits over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but the amount of data involved here is deliberately kept to the minimum needed to talk to you.

09Third-Party Links

Links on this site can take you to external services — for example WhatsApp (Meta) if you choose to message me there, or the Zstore AI Hebrew site. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply the moment you leave this site.
