Questions, answered by me.

Didn’t find yours? Ask me directly.

What does a typical timeline look like?

A focused landing page is usually live in one to two weeks. A complete site tends to run four to six, depending on how much content exists and what we connect. You’ll see the dates in your proposal before we start.

How is the price decided?

Every project is quoted as one fixed price for a written scope — no hourly meter running. We agree on what’s included first, and if the scope grows later, we agree on that too before anything changes.

My site exists but feels dated. Can you rework it?

Yes — that’s often the smartest project. I look at what’s already earning its keep, then propose either a targeted redesign or a fresh build, with an honest recommendation on which one your goals actually need.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

If you want to manage your own text, images or posts, I build the site on an editing setup that fits you and show you how to use it. You’ll be updating pages confidently from day one.

What support comes after going live?

The first 30 days of care are included: fixes, small adjustments and answers whenever you need them. After that, many clients keep a light monthly arrangement, and some simply call me when something new comes up.

I’m in the US — how does working across time zones go?

Smoothly. Most of the work moves asynchronously, so progress lands in your inbox while you sleep, and I keep hours free for live calls with US time zones. Everything happens in English or Hebrew, whichever you prefer.
