— 06 / FAQ

Most-asked, plainly answered.

No legalese, no fluff. If you don't see your question, just write me — I answer everything personally.

How long does a project usually take?
Most landing pages ship in 1–2 weeks. Full marketing sites land between 4–6 weeks depending on copy, asset readiness, and integration depth. You'll see a precise timeline in your proposal.
How do you price?
Fixed-price by project, never by the hour. Once we agree on scope, the number is the number — no surprises, no padded invoices, no scope-creep tax.
Do you use templates or page builders?
Almost never. Every site I ship is hand-coded so it's fast, custom, and future-proof. If a CMS is required, I build a custom WordPress theme — not pre-fab blocks.
Will my site be fast and SEO-friendly?
That's the baseline, not the upgrade. Every project ships with semantic HTML, accessibility, performance budgets, structured data, and Core Web Vitals tuning.
What about after launch?
You get 30 days of post-launch care included, free. After that, lightweight care plans are available — but most clients stay self-sufficient. The handoff is real.
Where are you based?
Tel Aviv, Israel. I work with clients worldwide and run async by default — though I'll happily jump on a call when it actually moves the project forward.
